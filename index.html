Open your phone and look at your screen time. The 30 minutes are already there. They are just going somewhere else.

How much time does it take?
30 to 60 minutes of reading a day, and one group call a week. The calls are an hour, and you can leave early.

I've tried to build a reading habit before. Why would this work?
Before, you were on your own. This time you have a plan built for you, a page goal that fits your day, a coach who texts you, and a group that meets twice a week.
I already read a lot. Will I get anything out of it?
